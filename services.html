Services

Practical support designed around safety

Each service area is organized to help users quickly identify the kind of support they need and take a next step with confidence.

Counselling Support

Confidential sessions, emotional care planning, trauma-informed listening, and referrals.

Legal Guidance

Simple explanations of rights, legal options, documentation support, and advisor access.

Emergency Help

Fast access to urgent actions, SOS support, hotline details, and immediate next steps.

Safe Reporting

Private reporting forms that allow users to document concerns and request follow-up support.

Mental Health Resources

Wellbeing guidance, stress regulation resources, coping tools, and trusted educational materials.

Shelter / Support Services

Placeholder shelter information, referral pathways, and coordinated access to safe spaces.

SafeHaven

A digital support portal designed for safety, dignity, and equal access to help.
